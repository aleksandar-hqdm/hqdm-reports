The frame

Surfpoint has the best reputation and accreditation in its Brooklyn set and rising leads; the lever is making the website match that strength with proper markup, surfaced credentials, and stronger service pages.

Surfpoint is the strongest account in this set on the signals that matter most for a Brooklyn detox and rehab: the best Google rating of its peers (4.8 on 126 reviews, climbing), a CARF Center of Excellence accreditation, and form leads that have climbed sharply to record levels this spring. Organic search grew dramatically into early 2026 before easing in April, a broad visibility shift that mostly affected informational content rather than the core pages. The gap is that the website does not yet match the profile's strength: the service and admission pages carry no structured data, one service page is missing its search snippet, and the strong accreditation is underused on the site. The plan makes the website match the profile, surfaces the trust signals, strengthens the core pages for conversion, and keeps an eye on the April easing.

Where the opportunity lives

A best-in-set profile sits on a website missing the markup, the snippet, and the surfaced credentials that would convert that strength.
Learn moreShow less▾
  • No structured data on inner pages. Only the homepage carries markup; the service and admission pages have none, so Google cannot read them as treatment offerings.
  • A missing search snippet. The alcohol-detox service page has no meta description, so Google writes its own, which weakens the click.
  • Underused accreditation. The CARF Center of Excellence and OASAS licensing are exactly the trust signals families look for, and they are not surfaced prominently on the site.
  • An April visibility easing. Traffic pulled back in April after a strong climb. It mostly hit informational content, but the core pages should be protected and strengthened.

The strategies

1

Make the website match the profile

the prerequisite
Add structured data to the inner pages, fill in the missing search snippet, and strengthen the service pages for conversion.
Learn moreShow less▾
What
Roll Service, MedicalBusiness, and FAQ structured data across the service and admission pages, add the missing meta description on the alcohol-detox page, and strengthen each service page with depth and a clear intake call to action.
Why
The profile is the strongest in its set, but the website that backs it carries no markup on the inner pages and a missing snippet, so it under-converts the reputation lead. These are fixes to pages that already rank and get traffic.
How
Build one markup and intake template and roll it across the service and admission pages; write the missing snippet in the same pass.
2

Surface the accreditation

trust
Put the CARF Center of Excellence and OASAS credentials prominently on the site, where families look for them.
Learn moreShow less▾
What
Surface the CARF Center of Excellence accreditation and OASAS licensing prominently on the homepage, admissions, and service pages, with the accreditation reflected in the page markup.
Why
Accreditation is one of the strongest trust signals in this category, most competitors do not carry it, and Surfpoint underuses it on the site today. It directly supports conversion on a high-stakes decision.
How
Add a clear credentials block to the key pages and include the accreditation in the structured data.
3

Watch the April easing; tidy the thin pages

protect + discipline
Review the April visibility pullback, protect the core pages, and tidy the thin neighborhood pages instead of adding more.
Learn moreShow less▾
What
Review which content lost visibility in April, confirm the core detox and rehab pages held, and work through the thin neighborhood pages one at a time (refresh, merge, or a small redirect).
Why
The April easing mostly hit broad informational content, which is volatile and does not convert. Protecting the core pages and tidying the thin set concentrates value on the pages that matter.
How
Compare the pre- and post-April top pages, shore up any core page that slipped, and handle the thin pages with care, never a bulk removal.
4

Keep the profile aligned; clean measurement

upkeep
Keep the Google profile services aligned to the rebuilt pages, and report form and phone leads consistently.
Learn moreShow less▾
What
Keep the Google profile's services pointed at the rebuilt pages, and report leads as form submissions plus phone taps, split by channel.
Why
The profile is the lead engine; keeping it aligned to clean pages compounds the conversion work, and a consistent lead definition keeps the monthly read trustworthy.
How
Sync the profile services after each page ships and standardize the monthly lead report.
