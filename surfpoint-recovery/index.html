Overview
  • Organic search grew dramatically into early 2026: clicks rose from the 1,500-a-month range in spring 2025 to a peak of about 7,500 in March 2026.
  • In April that visibility pulled back sharply (to around 2,000 a month), a broad search-visibility shift that mostly affected informational content rather than the core pages.
  • The lever is the website: service and admission pages carry no structured data, and one service page is missing its search snippet (meta description).

What this means

The big picture is healthy: form leads are at record levels and the profile leads its Brooklyn set on reputation. The April traffic easing is worth watching, but it landed mostly on broad informational content, not the pages that convert. The durable gain is on the website itself: adding the page markup that is missing on the service and admission pages, and filling in the search snippet on the service page that lacks one.
