Overview
  • Organic visits from Google are up about 18% year over year, with a strong run through the winter (peaking near 3,500 visits a month in January and February) and a normal spring easing.

What this means

Visits are healthy and up year over year, and the studio is seen far more often in Google than a year ago. The opportunity is click capture: the site appears a lot but earns a small share of the clicks, partly because the service pages are coded too generically for Google to tell them apart. Sharpening the page titles and the page markup is where the next gain comes from, which is exactly where the plan focuses.
