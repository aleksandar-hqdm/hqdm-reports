Overview
  • On Google Maps across Santa Clarita, AQMS holds a top-3 spot for the core moving searches, moving company, moving company near me, moving services, and local movers, across most of the grid. Organic search also reached its strongest month in May, 470 visits, up 47% over April and more than triple where it stood in March.

Why Google Maps matters most here

Google Maps is how most people find a local mover, and it is where AQMS is strongest, sitting at the top of the map across most of the Santa Clarita Valley and ahead of the nearby competition. Holding and extending that position is the single highest-value local-search priority.
