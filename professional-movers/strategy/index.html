The short version

Where this brand stands now, and what to focus on next.
  • They are the biggest, most trusted mover in their home area: 537 reviews at 4.9 stars and the #1 spot on Google Maps at home. The job is to protect this.
  • Last September their Google rankings dropped in an algorithmic update. They have been recovering since February, and the fastest path back is to deepen the pages that already rank.
  • The fix is to deepen the pages that already rank and keep the home-market Google profile strong.
  • Make the main service pages (piano, hot tub, office moving) as complete as their best page, because those are the pages that win customers.
  • Their "how to pack" guides get lots of readers but few become jobs. Link those guides to the pages where people actually book a move.

The frame

Professional Movers already wins at home, so the plan is recovery, not new territory: clear out what is dragging the site down, deepen the service pages that already win, and keep defending the #1 map position.

Professional Movers has the assets a winner needs. A 45-year Detroit-area brand, full carrier credentials (USDOT, MCICC, CVED), "Best Moving Company" press recognition, the #1 Google Maps position in its home market, and 537 reviews at 4.9, far ahead of any nearby local mover. The service pages still carry most of the search value. What hurt the site was a search-ranking drop in September 2025, lining up with a Google update, after a batch of thin templated suburb pages and a stack of low-quality links were added too fast. So the play is not new territory. It is recovery: remove what is dragging the site down, deepen the pages that already win, and keep the Google profile and the guide content strong. All map work stays on the single managed Walled Lake pin.

Search volumes referenced are DataForSEO estimates, not literal counts. Professional Movers runs three offices but HQDM manages one Google Business Profile (Walled Lake HQ); all profile work is that single pin, not new locations.

The competitive picture

Two different games: at home on the map Professional Movers dominates with no real local-pack rival; in regular Google search the competition is national van lines, so the service pages have to match national-grade depth.
Learn moreShow less▾

There are two different competitive games, and they call for two different plays:

  • On the map, in the home market, Professional Movers dominates. At the Walled Lake location it holds #1 on every tracked search. The nearest real local mover carries about 23 reviews at 4.3, against 537 at 4.9. The other movers in the pack rank well below Professional Movers on reviews and rating, so there is no serious local-pack challenger at home.
  • In regular Google search, the rivals are national. The site competes with national van lines and comparison platforms (Two Men and a Truck, HireAHelper, Allied, All My Sons, MoveAdvisor), not the local movers. That means the service pages have to match national-grade depth to win back rankings.
  • The strongest existing page, the long-distance moving page, is the model: full credentials, a clear step-by-step process, pricing options, and valuation detail. The other service pages need to reach that same depth.

Measurement and framing

Leads are website form enquiries, and with no call tracking installed the true total is higher; lead the client story on the recovery since February, not a single year-over-year number.
Learn moreShow less▾
  • Leads. Leads are website form enquiries from analytics (the form-submit event). There is no call tracking installed for this account, so phone calls are not counted; for a mover, calls are usually a large share, which means the true lead total is higher than what is shown.
  • The honest year-over-year read. On a like-for-like basis, total form enquiries are down modestly versus a year ago, in line with the traffic drop, but they held up far better than traffic and have been recovering since February (42 to 101 by May, after stripping out job applications and the corporate relocation portals). Lead the client story on that recovery, not on a single year-over-year total.
  • Organic search is the bright spot. Once job applications and the corporate relocation portals (HFHS, Cardinal Health, Ascension) are excluded, organic search drives about three-quarters of the real form leads and is recovering in 2026. Phone calls from the Google profile are not tracked, so the profile's true lead contribution is larger than forms alone show.
  • Scope. One managed Google profile (Walled Lake). No multi-location or new-area framing in client comms; the other offices' profiles are not under management.

What to track

  • Service-page rankings in the positions 11 to 50 band, the pages where depth work pays back first.
  • The lift from the guides to the service pages as the internal links go in, the signal that reading traffic is converting.
  • The home-area map position and the lead recovery month over month.

The strategies

1

Recover and deepen the pages that already win

highest return
Deepen the service pages sitting just below page one; this is the fastest, lowest-risk path back.
Learn moreShow less▾
What
Deepen the priority service pages currently sitting just below page one (hot tub, piano, artwork, senior, office, local moving). Refocus the homepage on the brand and the core services. Route internal links and structured data to the priority pages that still lack them.
Why
Service pages already carry about half the site's search clicks, and a large band of their keywords sits at positions 11 to 50, where small gains turn into traffic. This is the fastest, lowest-risk path back.
How
  • Bring each priority service page up to the depth of the long-distance page: process, credentials, pricing, specialty handling, photos.
  • On the suburb pages, lead each with genuinely local detail (neighborhood specifics, nearby landmarks) rather than templated copy.
  • Add links from the strong pages and the guides into the priority service pages, and add structured data at publish.
2

Strengthen the home-market Google profile

defend the #1
Keep the one managed Walled Lake profile fully built out and complete, since the map at home is the strongest asset and the highest-intent channel.
Learn moreShow less▾
What
Keep the single managed Walled Lake profile fully built out: complete "from the business" details and an accurate, locally anchored products and services list.
Why
The map position at home is the strongest asset and the highest-intent channel for a mover, and its phone calls are not even tracked, so it contributes more than the form numbers show. The job is to protect and reinforce it, not to chase new areas.
How
Work the one managed pin: fill in the blank profile sections and anchor the products to the home service area. Stay inside the managed pin's home radius.
3

Keep the content momentum and convert the readers

turn readers into enquiries
The how-to guides pull in a steady new audience but few reach a page that books a move; link each guide to its matching service page and add a few buyer-stage guides.
Learn moreShow less▾
What
Keep the "how to pack" and "how to move" guides going, since they are the fastest-growing content, and link each guide to the matching service page. The Detroit-area cost and what-to-look-for guides already exist, so link and strengthen those rather than rebuild them, and add a tipping guide if one is missing.
Why
The guides pull in a steady new audience but few of those readers reach a page that books a move. Linking them to the service pages turns reading traffic into enquiry traffic, and the buyer-stage guides catch people closer to choosing a mover.
How
Add in-body links from the top guides to the matching service pages (pool table to piano, dishes to packing, fridge to local moving). Link the existing buyer-stage guides (moving cost, what to look for) to the service pages, and add a tipping guide if one does not already exist.
4

Own the high-value specialty moves

high value, low volume
Professional Movers already ranks for the specialty moves (hot tub, piano, pool table, artwork, the LCD and plasma TV page). Make that set the best in the market, and add a high-value occasion page only where demand is real and nothing covers it yet. Low volume, high intent, worth it on a few jobs a year.

What
Treat the specialty-move pages as their own high-value line: hot tub, piano, pool table, artwork, and the LCD and plasma TV page that is already a top gainer. Deepen each into the best page in the market, and add a new one (corporate and employee relocation, which the existing corporate accounts already prove demand for) only where it is real and not already covered.
Why
These searches do not show big volume, but the intent and the per-job value are high, so even a few jobs a year from one page pays off, and they are exactly the pages already recovering. Because this account is in recovery, the rule is to deepen and own the few that win, not mass-produce thin new pages, since thin pages are part of what caused the drop.
How
  • Make the existing specialty pages the best in the market: real detail, photos, internal links, and a clear quote call to action.
  • Add a new specialty or occasion page only where demand is real and nothing covers it (corporate and employee relocation is the clearest); check the live site and Asana first.
  • Keep each page unique and substantial; do not template or duplicate, especially while the site is recovering.
  • Judge these by jobs booked, not by traffic.
A note on framing

This account is recoverable without building authority from scratch. The brand, the reviews, the credentials, and the service pages are already there and already winning at home. Deepen what already ranks, keep the home-market profile strong, and convert the guide audience into enquiries, and the assets that are already there start working again.
