Overview
  • Organic clicks softened through 2025 and bottomed in February 2026, then rebounded strongly: May clicks were roughly 4× the February low.
  • Year over year, organic clicks are still down (the first quarter of 2026 ran below the same quarter of 2025), so the recovery is recent and worth protecting.
  • The clearest opportunity is on the website itself: service and location pages rank but earn very few clicks, and most inner pages carry no structured data for Google to read.

What this means

Search traffic is recovering and leads are growing, which is a healthy combination. The biggest remaining opportunity is click capture and conversion on the website: several Brooklyn searches rank near the top but earn almost no clicks (for example "recovery center brooklyn" showed in Google over 13,000 times in a recent window with effectively no clicks), and the service and admission pages carry no structured data. Fixing titles, snippets, and page markup turns rankings that already exist into visits and inquiries.
