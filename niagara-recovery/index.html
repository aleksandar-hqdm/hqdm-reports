Overview
  • Organic clicks have fallen sharply over the past year, from the thousands a month in early 2025 to the hundreds now. The drop is concentrated in broad informational articles that did not bring inquiries.
  • The site does not rank well for the core treatment searches: most of its remaining visibility is off-topic informational content, while the local "detox" and "rehab" service searches are thin.
  • A few site issues are capping the recovery, including an unfinished template placeholder on a service page and an inconsistent business name across the site (covered in the Work Plan).

What this means

The website's search traffic has dropped, but the local Google profile still owns the market, so the issue is the site, not the local position. The recovery is to fix the site foundation, make the business name consistent, clean up an unfinished page, add the page markup that is missing, and consolidate duplicate pages, then build the core treatment pages so the strong Maps presence finally converts into website inquiries.
