  • The big asset is Google Maps: the Newfane profile owns Western New York, top-3 on about 70% of the local map for the highest-intent searches, with eight nearby towns fully covered.

Niagara Recovery owns Western New York on Google Maps: it is top-3 on roughly 70% of the regional grid for the highest-intent searches, eight nearby towns are at full coverage, and branded search converts strongly. That local position is the account's biggest asset. The problem is that the website does not convert it. Organic traffic has fallen sharply over the past year (the drop is broad informational content, not the local position), web form leads are light, and a handful of fixable site issues are in the way: the site uses two different business names, an unfinished template placeholder is live on a service page, the service pages exist in duplicate versions, and the inner pages carry no structured data. The plan is foundation-first: make the brand name consistent, fix the unfinished and duplicate pages, add the missing markup, point the profile at the towns it already owns, and focus content on the core treatment pages so the strong Maps presence finally converts.

Where the opportunity lives

A strong Maps position sits on a website with fixable foundation problems: a split brand name, an unfinished page, duplicate service pages, and no structured data on inner pages.
Learn moreShow less▾
  • Two business names. The site brands itself "Niagara Recovery" in some places and "Recovery Center of Niagara" in others, which splits branded search demand and confuses the entity Google sees.
  • An unfinished page is live. A service page still shows a template placeholder where the phone number should be, a visible quality problem on a high-stakes page.
  • Duplicate service pages. Each service exists as two near-identical pages, which split ranking signals.
  • No structured data on inner pages. Only the homepage carries markup, so the treatment pages forfeit rich-result eligibility, and one location page describes a facility the business does not have.

The strategies

1

Fix the brand and site foundation

the prerequisite
Consolidate to one business name, fix the unfinished page, and add structured data to the inner pages.
Learn moreShow less▾
What
Make the business name consistent ("Niagara Recovery") across the whole site, replace the unfinished template placeholder on the service page, and roll Service, MedicalBusiness, and FAQ structured data across the inner pages (the homepage has markup; they do not).
Why
The split name divides branded demand and entity signals, the placeholder is a visible quality problem on a high-stakes page, and the missing markup forfeits rich-result eligibility. These are the foundation that everything else depends on.
How
Do a sitewide name pass, fix the placeholder, and roll one markup template across the inner pages.
2

Consolidate the duplicate service pages

stop splitting signals
Merge each service's two near-identical pages into one, and correct the location page that names a facility the business does not have.
Learn moreShow less▾
What
Consolidate the duplicate service pages down to one strong page per service (with a redirect), and correct the location page that describes a facility the business does not operate.
Why
Each service competing with its own duplicate splits ranking signals, and a page naming a non-existent facility is an accuracy and trust risk on a regulated service.
How
Keep the stronger page per service, redirect the weaker, and rewrite the location page to the real Newfane facility.
3

Point the profile at the towns it owns; defend the pin

the lead engine
Set the Google profile's service area to the nearby towns Niagara Recovery already dominates, and keep it aligned to the rebuilt pages.
Learn moreShow less▾
What
Set the Google profile's service-area list to the nearby towns it already wins in Maps (Newfane, Olcott, Lockport, and the anchor towns), remove the redundant entries, and keep the profile's services pointed at the rebuilt pages.
Why
The profile is the account's strongest asset and drives the path to the site. Aligning its service area to the real catchment and connecting it to clean pages compounds that strength.
How
Update the service-area list and re-point the profile services once the pages are consolidated.
4

Focus content on the core treatment pages

page discipline
Pause the broad article output and put the effort into the detox and rehab pages that convert.
Learn moreShow less▾
What
Pause the broad informational article output and redirect the effort into strengthening the core detox and rehab pages, which barely rank today.
Why
The broad articles brought traffic that fell away and never converted, while the pages that should win local treatment intent are thin. Fewer, stronger, on-theme pages is the better use of effort.
How
Hold new broad articles, rebuild the core treatment pages with depth and the new markup, and link them from the profile and the homepage.
