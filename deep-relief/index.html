Holding and extending this position is the single highest-value local-search priority.

The focus this quarter

The plan centers on the two highest-value moves. First, fill out the service pages and link them together, so the right page ranks instead of your homepage, which takes about 78% of your search clicks today. Second, build the high-intent pages people actually search for, like couples massage, and rebuild the Oak Hill page that was removed. The Business Profile fixes, page markup, and lead tracking support those two. Open a group to see its tasks, and tap “Why” on any task for the reasoning.

Our recommendations · from the custom strategy

These come from the June custom strategy. The service-page depth, internal linking, and the new high-intent pages are the highest-value, and the markup and tracking fold into the existing service-page work.
