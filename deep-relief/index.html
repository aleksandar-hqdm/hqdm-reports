Why Maps matters most here

Google Maps is how most people find a local clinic, and it is where Deep Relief is strongest. When someone at or near the clinic searches, you sit at the top of the map for nearly every service you offer, ahead of the nearby competition on most of them. Holding and extending this position is the single highest-value local search priority.
