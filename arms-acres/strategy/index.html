The short version

Where Arms Acres stands now, and what to focus on next.
  • The search foundation is strong and improving: the Carmel profile dominates Google Maps across Putnam County, and organic clicks are up about 76% year over year with rankings climbing into the single digits.
  • The single biggest lead win is on the website: the service and program pages take about 24,000 visits a year but have no contact form, only a call button, so they convert almost no inquiries. Add a form there.
  • Fix the homepage social-share preview, which currently shows another brand's text, and roll proper structured data across the main pages (only the homepage has any today).
  • A cluster of insurance and cost articles ranks just below the top of Google and earns very little. Rewriting their titles and adding FAQs is a quick click gain.
  • Defend the Carmel profile with a full optimization pass, and add the broad state-level rehab and detox pages the site is missing.

The frame

Arms Acres already wins Maps and has recovered strongly on organic; the growth lever now is converting that traffic on the website (forms, structured data, click capture), not chasing more visits.
Learn moreShow less▾

The Carmel inpatient profile dominates the Putnam County map grid and is still gaining, and organic search has recovered strongly: first-quarter clicks are up about 76% year over year and rankings have climbed from the low-20s into the single digits. So the search foundation is in good shape. The ceiling is conversion. The service and program pages take roughly 24,000 visits a year but carry only a call button, no form, so they convert almost none of that traffic, while the homepage, contact, and admissions pages do nearly all the converting. On top of that, the main pages carry almost no structured data, and a large insurance and cost article cluster ranks just below the top with very low click-through. The plan fixes the conversion mechanism, rolls out structured data, captures the insurance-cluster clicks, ships the broad pages the site is missing, and defends the Carmel pin, all on the single managed location.

All Maps work is the single managed Carmel pin. Search volumes are DataForSEO estimates. The rating is the client's own lever and is shown only as context.

Where the opportunity lives

The traffic is strong but under-converts: high-traffic service pages have no form, the main pages have no structured data, and a high-value article cluster ranks but earns few clicks.
Learn moreShow less▾
  • No form on the service pages. About 24,000 visits a year land on the service and program pages, which carry only a call button. Almost all current form leads come from the homepage, contact, and admissions pages instead. A form on the service pages is the single biggest lead win.
  • Structured data on the homepage only. The inpatient, detox, and service pages carry no markup, so they forfeit rich-result and AI-citation eligibility on exactly the pages that should rank for treatment intent.
  • A wrong-brand share preview. The homepage social-share text currently shows another brand's name, a straightforward site-quality issue to correct.
  • Insurance cluster ranks but earns little. A set of insurance, payor, and cost articles ranks just below the top of Google with very low click-through. Better titles and FAQs on pages that already rank is a low-effort gain.

The strategies

1

Add the service-page conversion mechanism

the biggest lead win
Put a real contact form on the service and program pages, which take about 24,000 visits a year today with no form.
Learn moreShow less▾
What
Add a clear contact and intake form to the service and program pages (inpatient, detox, adult rehabilitation, and the service pages), alongside the existing call button.
Why
These pages take roughly 24,000 visits a year and convert almost none, because there is no form to fill out. Today nearly all form leads come from the homepage, contact, and admissions pages. Adding a form to the pages that already get the treatment-intent traffic is the single highest-return change available.
How
Add one consistent intake form block to the service-page template, keep the call button, and confirm it reports as a lead in Analytics.
2

Roll out structured data and fix the share preview

site quality
Add LocalBusiness, Service, and FAQ markup across the main pages, and correct the homepage social-share text that shows another brand.
Learn moreShow less▾
What
Deploy LocalBusiness, Service, and FAQ structured data across the inpatient, detox, and service pages (the homepage carries only a basic Organization tag today), and replace the homepage social-share description that currently shows another brand's text.
Why
The pages that should rank for treatment intent carry no markup, so they miss rich-result and AI-citation eligibility. The wrong-brand share preview is a visible site-quality problem. Both are one-time fixes on pages that already perform.
How
Build one markup template and roll it across the Tier-1 pages; correct the share-preview text in the same pass.
3

Capture the insurance-cluster clicks

click capture
Rewrite titles and add FAQs on the insurance, payor, and cost articles that rank just below the top of Google.
Learn moreShow less▾
What
Rewrite the titles and add FAQ sections on the cluster of insurance, payor, and cost articles that rank on the second page of Google with very low click-through.
Why
These are high-intent searches (insurance and cost are the questions that precede a call), the pages already rank, and the click-through is far below what a top-three position earns. It is a low-effort lift on existing assets.
How
Prioritize by impressions, rewrite for a clearer promise, add the FAQ content and markup, and track the click-through lift.
4

Ship the broad pages, defend the Carmel pin

coverage + the lead engine
Add the state-level rehab and detox pages the site is missing, and run a full optimization pass on the managed Carmel profile.
Learn moreShow less▾
What
Ship the broad service pages (a state-level alcohol-rehab and detox page) the site does not have today, and run a full optimization pass on the managed Carmel Google profile.
Why
The site ranks well for brand and near-me searches but lacks pages for the broad state-level cluster, and the Carmel profile is the lead engine that should be kept fully optimized while it leads its market.
How
Build the broad pages with the new markup and form template, and complete the profile optimization (categories, services, details) on the managed Carmel pin.
