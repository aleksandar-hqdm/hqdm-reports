The short version

Where Elev8 Centers stands now, and what to focus on next.
  • The profile is strong: 4.5 stars on 101 reviews at the Harlem location, plus a strong accreditation set (OASAS, Joint Commission, LegitScript, NAATP), and the homepage is richly marked up.
  • Leads ramped to a March peak (121) then eased with an April search-visibility dip that mostly hit broad informational content.
  • The inner pages do not match the homepage: the treatment and admission pages carry no markup, and an expected treatment address returns an error, so the URL structure needs cleanup.
  • Bring the inner pages up to the homepage's standard and fix the treatment URLs. That is the structural fix that steadies the line after the April dip.
  • Focus the content on treatment topics rather than broad articles, and reflect the accreditations on the key pages and in the markup.

Where the opportunity lives

A strong profile and homepage sit above inner pages that lack markup and a clean URL structure, and a content mix tilted toward volatile informational topics.
Learn moreShow less▾
  • Markup stops at the homepage. The homepage is richly marked up, but the treatment and admission pages carry none, so they miss rich-result eligibility on exactly the pages that should win treatment intent.
  • A broken treatment URL. An expected treatment address returns an error and the live treatment pages sit under a different structure, which costs clarity and internal links.
  • Volatile content mix. Most of what the site ranks for is broad informational content, which is what moved in the April dip; the treatment pages barely rank.
  • Underused accreditation in markup. The strong accreditation set is a trust asset that is not fully reflected on the key pages or in the structured data.

The strategies

1

Bring the inner pages up to the homepage standard

the prerequisite
Extend the homepage's markup to the treatment and admission pages, and fix the treatment URL structure.
Learn moreShow less▾
What
Roll the Service, MedicalBusiness, and FAQ markup the homepage already has across the treatment and admission pages, and fix the treatment URL structure, including the expected address that returns an error.
Why
The homepage is well built, but the pages that should win treatment intent carry no markup and sit on a broken URL structure, which caps how they rank and link. This is finishing what the homepage started, not a rebuild.
How
Reuse the homepage markup as a template across the inner pages, repair the broken address with a redirect, and align the live treatment URLs into one clean structure.
2

Strengthen the treatment pages for conversion

convert the visits
Give the treatment pages depth, a clear intake call to action, and the accreditations families look for.
Learn moreShow less▾
What
Strengthen the treatment pages with real depth, a clear intake call to action, and the accreditations surfaced, and reflect those accreditations in the page markup.
Why
The profile and credentials are strong, but the treatment pages are thin and barely rank, so they under-convert the visits they get. Accreditation is a top trust signal on this decision and is underused on the pages today.
How
Build out each treatment page, add the intake call to action, and add a clear credentials block reflected in the structured data.
3

Recover the April dip; focus the content

protect + discipline
Review what lost visibility in April, protect the core pages, and focus the content program on treatment topics.
Learn moreShow less▾
What
Review which content lost visibility in April, confirm the core treatment pages held, and steer the content program toward treatment topics rather than broad informational articles.
Why
The April dip mostly hit broad articles, which are volatile and do not convert. Protecting the core pages and shifting content toward treatment intent makes the traffic that remains more durable and more valuable.
How
Compare the pre- and post-April top pages, shore up any core page that slipped, and re-weight the content calendar toward treatment topics.
4

Keep the profile aligned; clean measurement

upkeep
Keep the Harlem profile aligned to the rebuilt pages, and report leads consistently.
Learn moreShow less▾
What
Keep the managed Harlem profile's services pointed at the rebuilt pages, and report leads as form submissions by channel with a consistent definition.
Why
The profile is the lead engine; aligning it to clean pages compounds the conversion work, and a consistent lead definition keeps the monthly read trustworthy through the recovery from the April dip.
How
Sync the profile services after each page ships and standardize the monthly lead report.
