Overview
  • Organic clicks from Google reached a 12-month high in May (139), holding steady at roughly 100 to 140 a month across the year.
  • The number of times the site appeared in Google grew sharply, from about 13,000 in May 2025 to 88,000 in May 2026. Most of that new visibility is on informational blog topics, so clicks did not rise in proportion.
  • The commercial wins are concentrated: brand searches and Chino Hills "movers" terms rank on page one, with Tustin terms just behind.

What this means

Visibility is up a lot and clicks are at a 12-month high, which is a healthy direction. The gap between the two (far more appearances than clicks) is because much of the new visibility is on informational blog searches rather than buyers. The highest-value focus is converting the commercial searches (the Chino Hills and Tustin "movers" terms) and the two Maps profiles, not chasing informational impressions.

Why Google Maps matters most here

Google Maps is how most people find a local mover, and it drives close to three quarters of the calls. Chino Hills already leads its market; Tustin is the recovery opportunity in a tougher one. Holding and extending these two profiles is the single highest-value local-search priority, and it is exactly what the website-connection work in the plan is meant to support.
