Hudson shows what is possible

Hudson leads the map for every search, all the way across its service area, not just next to the office. It does it with a strong review base (635) and a well-built city page. That is the model for lifting Roseville, Lake Elmo, and Bloomington across their wider areas too.

  • A combined 2,265 reviews at 4.9 to 5.0, among the strongest in these markets.

The newest market, the most room to grow

Eau Claire is the newest location and the one with the most room. Its profile already points to its own Eau Claire page, so the gap now is its review base (22, far behind the other markets) and the depth of that page. Growing the reviews, which you drive, and deepening the page is the path to lifting Eau Claire toward the others.

Where the focus goes

Your locations and reviews are strong, so the work is not a rebuild. It is sharpening a healthy site and concentrating its strength on the pages and searches that bring calls. The focus sits in five places:

A clean location and service structure

One strong page per city and service, with the overlapping and thin pages folded into it. Removing the duplicates that quietly compete with your best pages lets those pages rank higher.

Linking and local signals

Point the service pages down to each city, link nearby locations together, and add a live map and your reviews to each page so every page carries a clear local signal tied to its Google profile.

The wider service area, location by location

Win the popular “near me” searches further out from each office, the way Hudson already does. For Bloomington that includes a missing profile category; for Lake Elmo, recovering a removed page.

Eau Claire, the one weak market

Its profile already points to the right page, so the focus is deepening that page with the nearby towns and growing its reviews toward the level of your other locations.

Clearer reporting

Your report now shows tracked calls next to the Google dashboard figures, so a change in how Google counts profile views is never mistaken for a change in real business.
