Resolved June 2026 Bloomington & Hudson

“The Google interactions for our Bloomington and Hudson profiles are down year over year for May. What is behind it, and what else can we do for these two locations?”

The request

You flagged that in Google Business Profile, the May interactions for the Bloomington and Hudson locations (the views and clicks on each profile) are lower than the same month last year. You wanted to know what is driving the drop, and whether there is anything more we can do for these two profiles.

What the analysis showed
  • The year-over-year figure comes from Google's own profile dashboard. We cannot rebuild it on our side: call tracking and analytics for your account both started this spring, so there is no last-May figure in our systems to compare against.
  • Google has changed how it counts profile views and interactions over the past year, and more searches now end without anyone clicking into a profile. Those numbers are down for many businesses regardless of how they are doing, so a lower figure there does not mean fewer customers. The number that tracks real new business is your phone calls, recorded in CallRail.
  • Hudson is the strongest of your profiles. It sits at or near the top of the map across its area (about #1 to #2 on the main searches, easing toward the edges), holds 637 reviews against the nearest competitor's 74, and its profile details are complete.
  • Bloomington is #1 on the searches next to its office, but averages about #6 on the wider “near me” searches further out, and is not yet ranked for “local movers near me” there. On the website side, its page is up versus last May, both in visits and in how often it appears in Google.
  • The recent month-to-month dip in tracked calls (April to May) shows at all four established locations together, not just these two, so it points to the season and the first month of tracking rather than a problem with these profiles.
  • One clear fix: the Bloomington profile is missing the “Moving and storage service” category that Hudson and two of your other locations already carry. Adding it can help it show up for more searches.

The written answer

A short, ready reply for the client. Copy it, adjust the greeting, and send.

Subject: Bloomington and Hudson – what the year-over-year numbers mean, and our next steps

Hi team,

Here is the short answer on the Bloomington and Hudson profiles.

The year-over-year drop you are seeing is in Google's Business Profile dashboard, in the “interactions” and “views” figures. Here is what is worth knowing:

  • Google has changed how it counts those numbers over the past year, and more searches now end without anyone clicking into a profile. They are down for a lot of businesses, so a lower figure there does not mean fewer customers. Your real measure of new business is tracked phone calls, which we now record in CallRail.
  • On the measures we can check, both profiles are in good shape. Hudson sits at or near the top of its Google map across its area (about #1 to #2 on the main searches), with 637 reviews against the nearest competitor's 74. Bloomington is #1 on the searches next to its office, and its own page in Google is up versus last May. The room to grow is the wider area around the office, where the larger “near me” searches sit.

Our next steps:

  • Add the “Moving and storage service” category to the Bloomington profile. Hudson and two of your other locations already have it, and it helps a profile show up for more searches.
  • Keep building Bloomington's presence in the wider area around the office, where the larger “near me” searches sit, using the profile and the monthly work already in place.
  • Keep Hudson's profile active and protect its lead. From now on we will show your tracked calls next to the Google figures so the full picture is clear.

Happy to talk any of this through on our next call.

Best,
HQDM
