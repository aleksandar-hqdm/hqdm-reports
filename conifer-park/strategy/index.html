The short version

Where Conifer Park stands now, and what to focus on next.
  • The account is healthy where it counts: form leads are strong and growing (about 80 a month), organic search is the largest source of those leads, and the Glenville profile gained ground on every tracked Capital Region search.
  • The treatment pages are locked to a single-city web address that caps how widely they can rank. Freeing them up lets Glenville's authority carry them across the region.
  • The homepage is well marked up, but the inner treatment pages carry no structured data. Extending it to them is a straightforward win.
  • Clean up the duplicate and thin pages, the stray phone-link pattern, and old migration files left on the site.
  • Add the trust signals competitors show (accreditations and an insurance list), which are missing today, and refresh the top blog pages so they keep feeding the treatment pages.

The frame

Conifer Park is in good shape on the measures that matter: form leads are strong and growing, organic search is the largest source of those leads (unusual and healthy for this kind of account), and the Glenville profile gained top-3 map coverage on every tracked Capital Region search over the spring. Organic clicks are lower than a year ago, but that decline is concentrated in broad informational articles that brought traffic without inquiries, while rankings on the core treatment searches actually improved. So this is not a recovery-from-decline story, it is an unlock story. The treatment pages are tied to a single-city web address that limits how widely they can rank, most carry no structured data even though the homepage is richly marked up, and the site carries duplicate and thin pages plus old migration debris. The plan frees up and marks up the treatment pages, cleans the site, adds the trust signals competitors show, and keeps extending the Glenville profile.

Where the opportunity lives

Strong fundamentals are capped by structural issues on the treatment pages: a single-city web address, missing markup, and duplicate or thin pages around them.
Learn moreShow less▾
  • Single-city web address. The treatment pages all carry a "-in-glenville-ny" web address even though the services are offered region-wide, which locks their relevance to one city and caps how widely they rank.
  • Markup on the homepage only. The homepage is richly marked up, but the inpatient, detox, and other treatment pages carry no structured data, so they miss rich-result and AI-citation eligibility.
  • Duplicate and thin pages. The site carries duplicate service pages, thin auto-built pages, a stray phone-link pattern, and leftover migration files that dilute the strong pages.
  • Missing trust signals. Competitors show accreditations and an insurance list; Conifer Park's site shows neither prominently, which is a conversion-trust gap on a high-stakes decision.

The strategies

1

Free up and mark up the treatment pages

the unlock
Move the treatment pages off the single-city web address and add Service and FAQ structured data to them.
Learn moreShow less▾
What
Rewrite the treatment pages (detox, inpatient rehab, dual diagnosis) off the "-in-glenville-ny" web address into clean service addresses, and add Service and FAQ structured data to each (the homepage is richly marked up; these pages carry none).
Why
The single-city address caps how widely these pages can rank even though the services are offered region-wide, and the missing markup forfeits rich-result eligibility on exactly the pages that should win treatment intent. Freeing them up lets the site's Glenville authority carry them across the Capital Region.
How
Rewrite each page, redirect the old address to the new one, and roll one markup template across all of them.
2

Clean up the site

remove the drag
Consolidate duplicate and thin pages, fix the stray phone-link pattern, and clear old migration files.
Learn moreShow less▾
What
Consolidate the duplicate service pages and the thin auto-built pages (handled one at a time), fix the stray phone-link pattern, and clear the leftover migration files.
Why
These dilute the strong pages and add maintenance noise. Tidying them concentrates ranking signals on the pages that convert.
How
Work page by page (refresh, merge, or a small redirect), never a bulk removal, and confirm the redirects resolve cleanly.
3

Add trust signals; refresh the top blogs

conversion + upkeep
Surface accreditations and an insurance list, and refresh the top blog pages so they hold rankings and feed the treatment pages.
Learn moreShow less▾
What
Surface the accreditations and an insurance list on the site, and refresh the top-performing blog pages with current information and links into the treatment pages.
Why
Accreditation and insurance are the trust questions that precede a call, and competitors show them while Conifer Park does not. Refreshing the top blogs protects the rankings that still feed the site and routes that interest toward the pages that book intakes.
How
Add the trust content to the admissions and treatment pages, and fold the blog refreshes into the existing content cadence.
4

Defend and extend the Glenville profile

the lead engine
Keep the managed Glenville profile fully optimized and connected to the freed-up treatment pages.
Learn moreShow less▾
What
Run a full optimization pass on the managed Glenville profile (description, services list pointed at the right pages, attributes) and keep it aligned to the rebuilt treatment pages.
Why
The profile is gaining on every tracked search and drives the path to the site and the intake line. Keeping it optimized and pointed at the right pages compounds the unlock work.
How
Complete the profile pass and re-point the services to the freed-up pages once they ship.
