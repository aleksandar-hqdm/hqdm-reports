Overview
  • Organic clicks are lower than a year ago, but the decline is concentrated in informational blog articles that did not convert, not in the treatment pages.
  • At the same time, rankings improved: the average position moved from the low-20s in spring 2025 into the low-teens, and form leads grew, so the account is healthier than the click count alone suggests.
  • The opportunity is the treatment pages: they are locked to a single-city web address that caps them, and most carry no structured data.

What this means

The lower click count looks worse than the reality. The drop is in broad informational articles that brought traffic but not inquiries, while rankings on the core treatment searches improved and form leads grew. The next gain is freeing up the treatment pages, which are tied to a single-city address that limits how widely they can rank, and adding the page markup most of them are missing.
