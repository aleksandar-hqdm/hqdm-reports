Why Google Maps matters most here

Most people planning an event find a photo booth through Google Maps and local search. It is where booking intent is highest. MIA already holds a strong review base and a solid position in its home area. The biggest growth opportunity is becoming visible in the busy event areas of Miami, where most weddings, corporate events and parties happen.

The foundation in place
103 · 5.0 reviews · rating 2nd strongest review base Top local link & authority Since 2020 Miami brand

MIA's review base is the second largest of any photo booth company we track in Miami, and its website authority is ahead of most local rivals. These are exactly the signals Google rewards on the map. They are simply not yet pointed at the areas with the most events.

The opportunity · the Miami event corridor

The single highest-value move

The Google Business Profile is currently set up as a photography studio rather than a photo booth company. Every competitor that wins the map is categorised as a photo booth or event rental business. Correcting the profile category and focus is the cheapest, highest-return move available, and it is the first item in the work plan.
